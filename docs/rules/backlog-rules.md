# Backlog rules

**What the backlog is.** The backlog is the one file holding every question kept for later. It holds questions and nothing else.

**Who reads it.** The human, to pick a question up. /cycle-plan, to enter a question and to take one out. /record-sync, to repoint a record an entry cites. Nothing reads it to pick a question up on the human's behalf.

**Lifetime.** One file, `docs/backlog.md`, titled "# Backlog", created once by the human, never numbered, never deleted. With nothing to hold it reads "None." under the title and any canvas line.

**Entry.** One bullet per question. Its line opens with a label, B1 upward, one above the highest the file has ever held on any branch or in the history, never reused; then the question. Sub-bullets Reason and Owner, as the docket rules give them for an open question, then, for an entry with a drawing, Drawings, by number. No Needed by.

**Entering.** A question enters when a docket leaves it undecided, nothing needs its answer, and the human keeps it rather than drops it. The human may enter a question that never went through a docket.

**Leaving.** An entry leaves when its question is decided and a cycle file carries a task that delivers the decision, or when the human strikes it.

**Canvas.** The backlog has one canvas, named "<repository> backlog" after the repository's name, made once by whoever adds the first drawing, never deleted. It is linked on the line under the title as "Canvas: " and the link. The canvas is part of the backlog: whoever reads the backlog opens it too. Whoever writes to the backlog may add a drawing to it. Each drawing belongs to one entry, the one that names it. It carries a number, 1 upward, one above the highest `docs/backlog.md` has ever named on any branch or in the history, never reused; options drawn for its question are lettered as the docket rules letter them. A drawing enters with its entry or joins one already there, and leaves in the edit that removes its entry.

**Writing.** Write a question as the docket rules write an open question.
