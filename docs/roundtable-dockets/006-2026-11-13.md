## Code decisions

- CD1. `Scrim` in `frontend/src/components/ui/Scrim.tsx` takes a list of elements to leave lit in place of its one `anchor`, and cuts one hole per element, each with that element's own corners and each followed on every animation frame. A list of choices names its one control. The screen dimmed for a guest names the guest's banner and "Log in", and nothing else.
  - Facts: PDR 058 dims the whole screen but for the banner across the top and "Log in"; that banner is the guest's banner, `ExampleBanner`, and the connection banner dims with the page. ADR 133 has `Scrim` cut one hole, over the control its panel is anchored to. The guest's banner spans the column above the top bar, and "Log in" stands at the right of the top bar, on one row with the menu button, the logo and "+". `AppShell`'s header starts its own stacking context, beneath `Scrim`. A press inside a hole reaches the element beneath it and never the scrim. `Scrim` has three callers, `LogInPrompt`, `PickerCombobox` and `RatingChip`. `Banner` takes no ref.
  - Rejected: One hole over a box enclosing the banner and "Log in", because the box also holds the menu button, the logo and "+", which PDR 058 dims. The banner and "Log in" lifted above the scrim by `z-index`, because they sit inside the header's stacking context and cannot rise above a scrim outside it. A second scrim component with two holes, because the hole, the swallowed tap and the scroll lock would be written twice. The scrim beneath the header with a second dim layer inside the header, because two layers would be kept at one strength and opened and closed together, and the header would know about the dimmed screen.
  - Becomes true: The developer lights any number of elements on a dimmed screen by naming them, and writes no scrim. `Banner` and `ExampleBanner` take a ref, and `AppShell` holds the guest's banner's ref beside the one of "Log in". An element that is named and not mounted gets no hole and dims. The user's browser reads one box a frame for each lit element.
  - Drawings: 3, 4, 6, 7
- CD2. The component that dims the screen for a guest is `GuestScrim` in `frontend/src/components/shell/GuestScrim.tsx`, and the context that opens it is `GuestScrimContext` in `frontend/src/lib/viewer.ts`; no name in the code keeps "prompt" for either. `GuestScrim` is not a Radix Popover. While open it renders `Scrim` alone, at the stronger strength, with the guest's banner and "Log in" lit, and closes on any press anywhere and on Escape through its own two listeners on the document. `AppShell` mounts it once and closes it on a change of address. `useWriteGuard` keeps its name.
  - Facts: PDR 058 shows no sentence with the press, and has a tap anywhere but "Log in" bring the screen back. ADR 134 makes `LogInPrompt` a Radix Popover anchored to "Log in", whose content is the sentence alone. Radix's closing on Escape and on a press outside exist only while a `Popover.Content` is mounted. No handler of the app's closes `LogInPrompt` on a press on "Log in"; Radix's closing on a press outside does. A press on the lit banner passes through its hole and never reaches the scrim (CD1). `LogInPrompt` prevents `onOpenAutoFocus` and `onFocusOutside` so that focus stays on the pressed control. `frontend/package.json` lists `@radix-ui/react-dialog` and `@radix-ui/react-popover` and no other Radix package. Nothing opens the dimmed screen over a Radix dialog or list.
  - Rejected: A Radix Popover with an empty `Popover.Content`, because the developer would find a popover with no panel, anchored and positioned for nothing, and would keep both focus preventions. Radix's `DismissableLayer` listed as a third Radix package, because Radix does not document it as a part to build on, and an upgrade could change it with no notice. A Radix Dialog, because "Log in" would be dead beneath it. `AppShell` rendering `Scrim` itself with no component, because `AppShell` would hold the two listeners in an effect told when the screen is dimmed, beside the menu, two sheets, the change of viewer and the header's height. The name `LogInPrompt` kept, because the component shows no prompt. The name `GuestDimming`, because it hides that the component is `Scrim`. The name `DimmedScreen`, because a list of choices dims the screen too. The name `WriteRefusal`, because it says nothing of what is drawn.
  - Becomes true: The developer finds the screen dimmed for a guest in one file, with no anchor, no positioning and no focus prevention. The listeners live only while `GuestScrim` is open. A press on the banner, on "Log in" or on the dimming closes it, and a press on "Log in" still opens Clerk's dialog. An Escape pressed while it is open over a Radix dialog or list closes both.
  - Drawings: 3, 4, 6, 7
- CD3. `rate` in `RunCardView` in `frontend/src/pages/RunPage.tsx` does not go through `useWriteGuard()`: a guest's choice of a rating is written to the address as a user's is. A handler goes through the guard exactly when it starts a write, and "Next card" stays guarded.
  - Facts: PDR 058 shows a guest's chosen rating on the answer and dims the screen at "Next card". ADR 134 passes the choosing of a rating through the guard. A chosen rating is written only to the address, as a URL parameter tagged with its card, and sends no request. "Next card" goes through the guard and is `disabled` until every answer has a rating.
  - Rejected: The choosing of a rating kept behind the guard, because PDR 058 shows the chosen rating on the answer. A guest's ratings held in component state, because they would be lost on a reload and would need a second path beside the user's.
  - Becomes true: The developer guards a handler exactly when it starts a write, with no exception. A guest's ratings stay in the address through the dimmed screen and a reload, and "Next card" comes alive from them with no code of its own.
  - Drawings: 6
