## Open questions

- Q12. May a guest choose a rating for each answer on a run's card, with the screen dimming only on "Next card"?
  - Reason: PDR 058 dims the screen when a guest chooses a rating, and rejects ratings a guest can choose with the move to the next card dimming the screen, because the guest would learn after choosing that it does not count. ADR 134 follows it. Choosing a rating changes nothing in the example library; only "Next card" rates the card, which PDR 053 has tell a guest to log in.
